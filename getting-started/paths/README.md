# Paths

**Pick the path that matches what you're building. Each one answers the questions people in that role ask first and points to the guides that fit.**

* [AI developers](ai-developers.md): keep agent memory, datasets, and records on Filecoin.
* [Enterprise developers](enterprise-developers.md): keep auditable records and archive large datasets.
* [Storage platforms and resellers](storage-platforms.md): build and sell your own storage service on Filecoin.
* [Migrate from IPFS](migrate-from-ipfs.md): store your IPFS content on Filecoin and keep your CIDs.
* [Storage providers](storage-providers.md): earn by providing storage.
