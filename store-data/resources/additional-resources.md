# Additional resources

**Useful links for building on Filecoin Cloud: service status, where to get tokens, explorers, network details, and community tools.**

## Service status

Subscribe to the [Filecoin Cloud status page](https://status.filecoin.cloud) for planned maintenance, downtime and contract upgrades.

## Get tokens

You need FIL for transaction fees and USDFC to pay for storage.

| Token | Calibration testnet | Mainnet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FIL | [Calibration faucet](https://faucet.calibnet.chainsafe-fil.io/funds.html) | Buy on an exchange, or bridge from another chain with [Squid Router](https://app.squidrouter.com) |
| USDFC | [tUSDFC faucet](https://forest-explorer.chainsafe.dev/faucet/calibnet_usdfc), or mint with tFIL on [usdfc.net](https://app.usdfc.net) | Swap with [Squid Router](https://app.squidrouter.com), or mint with FIL as collateral on [usdfc.net](https://app.usdfc.net) |
