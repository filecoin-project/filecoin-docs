# Get FIL

**The most common way to get FIL is to buy it on an exchange. Before you withdraw to a wallet, check which address types the exchange supports.**

Do your own research before you choose an exchange or on-ramp.

## Exchanges

A cryptocurrency exchange lets you buy, sell and trade cryptocurrencies for other cryptocurrencies or for fiat currency such as USD, EUR or JPY. Exchanges differ in fees, security and the assets they list. They carry risks, including hacks and regulatory uncertainty, so protect your funds.

Sites such as [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/) and [CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com/currencies/filecoin/markets/) list which exchanges support FIL.

To buy FIL:

1. Create an account. Many exchanges require identity verification (KYC), which can take anywhere from a few minutes to a few days.
2. Add funds in your local currency.
3. Exchange them for FIL.

### Address compatibility

Some exchanges let you deposit and withdraw FIL to any [address type](../concepts/addresses.md). Others support only one or a few of them, and some do not yet support [`f410` addresses](../concepts/addresses.md#delegated-addresses-f4), the form behind `0x` addresses.

If your exchange can't withdraw to an `0x` address, relay the funds through a wallet that can. See [Transfer FIL](transfer-fil.md).

## Fiat on-ramps

A fiat on-ramp converts fiat currency into cryptocurrency without an exchange account. FIL is supported by on-ramps such as [Changelly](https://changelly.com/), [ChangeNOW](https://changenow.io/), [MoonPay](https://www.moonpay.com/), [Ramp Network](https://ramp.network/) and [Simplex](https://www.simplex.com/). To add another, [raise an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/filecoin-project/filecoin-docs/issues/new).

## Test FIL

To test an app on a testnet such as [Calibration](../networks/calibration.md), get test FIL (`tFIL`) from a [Calibration faucet](../networks/calibration.md#get-test-fil). It has no value.
